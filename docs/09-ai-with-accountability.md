# AI with accountability

Phantix includes AI — carefully.

---

## Our stance

| AI does | AI does not |
|---------|-------------|
| Explain findings in plain language | Invent vulnerabilities |
| Help triage and narrate | Own risk scores or compliance pass/fail |
| Plan multi-step investigations | Bypass dual-control |
| Suggest remediation themes | Replace scanners or human authorization |
| Learn reusable **skills** from success (anonymized) | Upload your raw findings into a public model dump |

**AI orchestrates. Engines execute. Evidence stays grounded.**

## Privacy for AI

- Prefer **local / controlled** models for routine tenant-touching work
- External models only with minimization and/or explicit approval paths
- Skill sharing strips tenant identifiers before any platform-level pattern library
- AI actions can be audited

Designed with privacy obligations such as **NDPA** in mind for Nigerian organizations.
