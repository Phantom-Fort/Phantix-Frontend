## Questions leaders usually ask

### “Will this expose our customer data?”

Phantix is built so **security evidence** (assets, scans, findings, risks) lives in a **dedicated security database you control**. The platform manages tenancy, identity, billing, and orchestration — not a free-for-all copy of your production business systems.

### “How do I know the report is real?”

Client-facing reports emphasize **verified** findings. Heuristic noise is held back or listed separately for transparency — not mixed into the executive narrative as if every line were confirmed.

### “What does Premium buy me?”

Continuous assurance: deeper assessment workflows (including VAPT campaigns), stronger reporting formats, AI-assisted explanations, richer alerts, and intelligence surfaces — under an active subscription. Free gets you started on surface visibility without a card.

### “Can we start without a big project?”

Yes. **Free** is designed for safe onboarding. You can grow into Premium and optional engagements (full VAPT, specialist work) when the business is ready.
